## Overview

A university cognitive-robotics project in which the Pepper robot acts as a mall assistant. It combines:

- **Perception** — person and face detection.
- **Dialogue** — a RASA chatbot.
- **Speech** — Whisper speech-to-text.
- **Robotics middleware** — ROS.

The system can run with the physical Pepper robot switched on or off.
